Talk at the [UCL Robotics Society](https://studentsunionucl.org/clubs-societies/robotics-society) on our research roadmap for humanoid interaction, from no contact to full physical contact.

The roadmap has three stages: humanoid–human interaction, where the robot's body motion influences a human partner without touching them; humanoid–static object interaction, where it acts on stationary objects through contact; and humanoid–dynamic object interaction, where it adapts in real time to moving objects.
