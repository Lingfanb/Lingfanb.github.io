Talk at [Physical I/O](https://www.physical-io.com/), London's physical AI community, as one of the speakers at [Episode 02: Robotics](https://www.physical-io.com/event-02-robotics).

The talk was about making humanoid motion expressive in the physical world. A familiar action such as a wave can be performed in many ways, and how a robot moves changes how people read it. I connected three of our papers: choosing a motion response that fits the interaction ([Hierarchical Intention-Aware Expressive Motion Generation](/publication/2025-hiear)), making the expression of a chosen action continuously adjustable through valence and arousal ([PAMoR](/publication/2026-pamor)), and preserving the motion's expressive characteristics when the real robot executes it ([Beyond Kinematic Tracking](/publication/2026-beyond-kinematic-tracking)).
